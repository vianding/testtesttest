Add a concise subsection covering the regulatory basis for haircuts on non-cash VM collateral across Canada, UK, EU and U.S. The purpose is to substantiate the paper's use of \(q_{\rm reg}\) and clearly distinguish it from \(q_{\rm CSA}\) and \(q_{\rm repo}\).

The subsection should answer:

Do applicable margin regulations prescribe or provide a methodology/minimum haircut for eligible non-cash collateral used as VM?

The answer is yes, subject to jurisdiction, entity/counterparty scope and collateral eligibility.

Use primary regulatory sources only, and quote the operative language briefly and exactly.

Canada — OSFI E-22, §4.2 Haircuts. Cite the provision stating:

“Any margin exchanged must be haircutted to account for potential changes in the value of the collateral.”

Also cite the provision stating:

“Otherwise, standard supervisory haircuts from the table below must be used.”

Explain that the standardized schedule gives a 4% haircut for qualifying AAA–AA− sovereign debt with residual maturity \(>5\) years, while an eligible model-based methodology may apply where permitted.

EU — Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/2251, Annex II. Cite the adjusted-collateral-value methodology

$$ C_{\rm value}=C(1-H_C-H_{FX}) $$

and the operative requirement that counterparties apply “at least the haircuts provided” in the relevant tables. Note the applicable 4% standardized haircut for the corresponding high-quality sovereign/government debt category with residual maturity \(>5\) years.

UK — UK EMIR margin RTS / retained and amended version of Delegated Regulation 2016/2251, Annex II. Cite the equivalent UK requirement that counterparties apply “at least the haircuts provided” in the tables, together with the corresponding 4% \(>5\)-year high-quality sovereign/government debt haircut. Use the current FCA/PRA/UK legislation source rather than treating the EU text itself as evidence of the current UK rule.

Cite the CFTC and/or U.S. prudential-regulator margin rules showing that, where non-cash collateral is eligible for VM, its value is subject to prescribed regulatory haircuts/discounts. Make the conceptual distinction explicit:

$$ \boxed{ q_{\rm CSA}\neq q_{\rm reg}\neq q_{\rm repo} } $$
\(q_{\rm CSA}\): contractual valuation percentage determining the collateral call under the CSA;
\(q_{\rm reg}\): regulatory recognition of eligible collateral after the applicable prudential haircut;
\(q_{\rm repo}\): market funding/advance rate obtainable by repoing the collateral.

The regulatory evidence supports \(q_{\rm reg}\); it does not imply anything about the contractual CSA haircut or the repo-market haircut.
