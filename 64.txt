# Objective

Before writing another line of code, I would like you to step back and evaluate the project as the lead quantitative architect.

Your job is **not** to immediately implement more features.

Instead, determine what the next stage of the library should be.

Assume the goal is to evolve this into a realistic institutional quantitative pricing framework while preserving:

* modularity
* financial interpretability
* extensibility
* backward compatibility
* research friendliness
