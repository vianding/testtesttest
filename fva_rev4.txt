1. Collateral process \(B_t\)

Early in the paper, preferably in Section 1 after the notation is introduced, define the bond collateral process explicitly as

$$ \boxed{ B_t=\min\left(\frac{V_t}{q_{\rm CSA}},B_t^{\rm cap}\right) } $$

where \(B_t^{\rm cap}\) is the maximum market value of eligible bond collateral available for posting from the relevant ring-fenced pool.

Explain briefly that this is one collateral process with two regimes:

$$ B_t= \begin{cases} V_t/q_{\rm CSA}, & V_t/q_{\rm CSA}\le B_t^{\rm cap},\\ B_t^{\rm cap}, & V_t/q_{\rm CSA}>B_t^{\rm cap}. \end{cases} $$

Before the cap binds, collateral follows the contractual CSA call; after the cap binds, available inventory constrains further posting.

Do not state that Proposition 2 requires \(B_t\) to be exogenous. The reduced-form identity holds for a general collateral process \(B_t\). What matters for \(q_{\rm reg}\)-invariance is whether \(q_{\rm reg}\) itself affects the process determining \(B_t\).

Under the stated process \(B_t=\min(V_t/q_{\rm CSA},B_t^{\rm cap})\), \(B_t\) depends on \(V_t,q_{\rm CSA}\) and the pool cap, but not directly on \(q_{\rm reg}\), provided the CSA-prescribed posting satisfies the applicable regulatory requirement. If regulatory requirements themselves force additional collateral posting, then \(q_{\rm reg}\) can affect total ColVA indirectly through

$$ q_{\rm reg}\rightarrow B_t\rightarrow F_t. $$

Keep this caveat concise in the main text; detailed discussion can go in the appendix.

2. Move Proposition 1 out of the main text

In §3.2, after defining

$$ A_t=\min(C_t,F_t),\qquad G_t=(C_t-F_t)^+,\qquad X_t=(F_t-C_t)^+, $$

$$ \boxed{ A_t+G_t+U_t=V_t, \qquad A_t+X_t=F_t } $$

and hence

$$ \boxed{X_t=F_t-A_t}. $$

Also state that at most one of \(G_t\) and \(X_t\) is non-zero.

Explain in one sentence that these identities fully allocate both the exposure \(V_t\) and available repo funding capacity \(F_t\).

Move the existing formal Proposition 1 and its proof to a new appendix. Add a short main-text cross-reference such as “A formal proof is given in Appendix A.”

3. Generalize Channel 3 using \(r_{\rm alt}\)

Revise §4.3 so Channel 3 is expressed generically using

$$ r_{\rm alt,t}, $$

rather than hard-coding \(r_{\rm CoF,t}\).

The key economic distinction should be clear:

Channels 1 and 2 concern funding of this trade's exposure, so their natural benchmark is \(r_{\rm base}\), the funding benchmark already embedded in base PV.
Channel 3 arises from incremental repo capacity beyond the collateral-supported portion. This introduces an opportunity-use question: what would the dealer otherwise do with that incremental cash?

Therefore write

$$ \boxed{ \mathrm{ColVA}_3= \int_0^T E_0\!\left[ DF_t^{\rm base} X_t(r_{\rm repo,t}-r_{\rm alt,t}) \right]dt } $$

subject to the optional-monetization treatment below.

Define \(r_{\rm alt}\) as the economic value of the best alternative use of incremental repo cash.

Do not mechanically identify \(r_{\rm alt}\) with \(r_{\rm base}\). Conceptually:

$$ r_{\rm base} =\text{benchmark embedded in this trade's base valuation}, $$

whereas

$$ r_{\rm alt} =\text{marginal economic value of incremental cash}. $$

They could coincide under a particular FTP framework, but there is no general reason they must.

4. Explain the systematic trade-level attribution issue concisely in §4.3

The methodology in this white paper is intended to be applied systematically across trades. Therefore, do not automatically give every individual trade credit for reducing unsecured funding elsewhere in the portfolio.

The appropriate \(r_{\rm alt}\) should be determined consistently from the relevant desk/Treasury funding state and FTP framework.

Distinguish two principal cases.

Marginally unsecured-funded desk

If incremental repo cash genuinely displaces marginal unsecured borrowing:

$$ \boxed{r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}}. $$

The Channel 3 benefit then represents avoided unsecured funding elsewhere.

Fully funded / cash-surplus desk

If the desk has no marginal unsecured funding need, excess cash should not receive an \(r_{\rm CoF}\) benefit. Its alternative value is the return available from parking/investing cash:

$$ r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm park}. $$

If \(r_{\rm cash}\) denotes the available cash/near-risk-free investment return, recognize that repoing the collateral merely to create excess cash is optional. The desk should not undertake the incremental repo if

$$ r_{\rm repo}\ge r_{\rm cash}. $$

Therefore the Channel 3 contribution for a fully funded desk can be represented as

$$ X_t\min(0,r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm cash}), $$

or equivalently using the effective benchmark

$$ \boxed{ r_{\rm park}=\max(r_{\rm cash},r_{\rm repo}) }. $$

Explain briefly that this max is an effective monetization benchmark, not a claim that cash can literally be invested at the repo rate. When repoing is uneconomic, the optimal action is simply not to repo the excess collateral.

A compact production rule may therefore be stated schematically as

$$ r_{\rm alt}= \begin{cases} r_{\rm CoF}, & \text{if incremental cash displaces marginal unsecured funding},\\[3pt] r_{\rm park}, & \text{if the desk is otherwise fully funded}. \end{cases} $$

Keep the main-text discussion concise. Detailed FTP, allocation and alternative-rate discussion belongs in the appendix.

6. Revise the reduced-form proposition

Retain the reduced-form result as an important economic result, not merely an algebraic check.

Define

$$ S_t=\min(V_t,F_t), \qquad U_t^f=(V_t-F_t)^+, \qquad X_t^f=(F_t-V_t)^+. $$

Emphasize their direct funding interpretation:

\(S_t\): amount of this trade's funding requirement met through repo;
\(U_t^f\): genuine residual funding shortfall;
\(X_t^f\): genuine repo cash in excess of this trade's total funding requirement.

Under the base-case assumption

$$ r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}, $$

the three-channel formulation reduces exactly to

$$ \boxed{ S_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) + U_t^f(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) + X_t^f(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm CoF}) }. $$

This should be presented as an exact equivalent representation under the stated assumption, not merely a validation check.

Its economic interpretation should be concise:

$$ F_t<V_t \Rightarrow \text{the residual shortfall is funded at }r_{\rm CoF}; $$ $$ F_t>V_t \Rightarrow \text{genuine excess repo cash is valued against its alternative use}. $$

Under \(r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}\), the latter is marginal unsecured funding displacement.

7. General \(r_{\rm alt}\) and the limits of the simple reduced form

Do not imply that the simple \(q_{\rm reg}\)-free reduced form is structural for arbitrary \(r_{\rm alt}\).

Starting from the full three-channel expression

$$ A_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) +(U_t+G_t)(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) +X_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm alt}), $$

show, either briefly in the reduced-form section or with derivation in the appendix, that for general \(r_{\rm alt}\) the exact representation contains the additional correction

$$ \boxed{ S_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) +U_t^f(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) +X_t^f(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm alt}) +(S_t-C_t)^+(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm alt}) }. $$

The final term reflects the fact that some \(X_t=(F_t-C_t)^+\) in the three-channel attribution may still be funding this trade's own \(U_t\); it is not yet genuinely excess to the trade. Genuine external excess is

$$ X_t^f=(F_t-V_t)^+. $$

When

$$ r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}, $$

the correction vanishes and the simple reduced form is recovered.

This is also why \(q_{\rm reg}\) disappears from the total under that base-case assumption, provided \(q_{\rm reg}\) does not independently alter \(B_t\). For general \(r_{\rm alt}\), \(C_t=\min(V_t,q_{\rm reg}B_t)\) remains relevant and the total may depend explicitly on \(q_{\rm reg}\).

Keep the main-text explanation short; place the full algebraic proof in the appendix.

8. Revise the existing “What this does and does not mean” discussion

Preserve the useful economic content but update it consistently with the above.

In particular:

\(q_{\rm reg}\) determines the collateral-recognition split and hence attribution across the three channels; do not describe \(U_t=(V_t-q_{\rm reg}B_t)^+\) as automatically equal to the net unsecured cash funding requirement. The latter is

$$ U_t^f=(V_t-F_t)^+. $$

The \(q_{\rm reg}\)-cancellation in the simple reduced form follows from the base-case economic assumption

$$ r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}. $$

The reduced-form identity does not require \(B_t\) to be exogenous. However, if \(q_{\rm reg}\) changes the collateral actually posted, it can affect total ColVA indirectly through

$$ q_{\rm reg}\rightarrow B_t\rightarrow F_t. $$

Where appropriate, mention the example \(q_{\rm CSA}>q_{\rm reg}\) as a situation in which regulatory requirements could potentially constrain the required collateral amount. Do not overstate this without contractual/regulatory support.

Retain the existing \(r_{\rm alt}\) sensitivity table, updated for the generalized framework. Introduce it as illustrating the interaction between \(q_{\rm reg}\) and the treatment of incremental repo capacity — not as illustrating every caveat above.
