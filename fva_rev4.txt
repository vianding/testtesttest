Please update the white paper so that Channel 3 is written generically using \(r_{\rm alt,t}\) rather than hard-coding \(r_{\rm CoF,t}\).

Define

$$ r_{\rm alt,t} $$

as the opportunity-cost / alternative-use rate applicable to incremental repo cash that is not required to finance the collateral-supported portion of this trade. The economic interpretation is:

\(r_{\rm base,t}\): benchmark already embedded in the base valuation of this trade;
\(r_{\rm CoF,t}\): dealer's marginal unsecured funding cost;
\(r_{\rm alt,t}\): value of the best alternative use of incremental repo cash.

The base-case assumption in this note remains

$$ r_{\rm alt,t}=r_{\rm CoF,t}, $$

corresponding to a dealer that is a marginal unsecured borrower and can use additional repo cash to displace unsecured funding elsewhere. However, keep \(r_{\rm alt}\) explicit in the general equations so the assumption is transparent rather than embedded in the Channel 3 formula.

Therefore write Channel 3 as

$$ \mathrm{ColVA}_3 = \int_0^T E_0\!\left[ DF_t^{\rm base}\, X_t (r_{\rm repo,t}-r_{\rm alt,t}) \right]dt, $$

subject to any existing optional-monetization convention in the note.

Also revise Proposition 2 carefully. Do not claim that the existing \(q_{\rm reg}\)-free reduced form holds for arbitrary \(r_{\rm alt}\).

Starting from

$$ C_t=\min(V_t,q_{\rm reg}B_t),\qquad F_t=\rho q_{\rm repo}B_t, $$ $$ A_t=\min(C_t,F_t),\quad G_t=(C_t-F_t)^+,\quad U_t=V_t-C_t,\quad X_t=(F_t-C_t)^+, $$

and

$$ A_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) +(U_t+G_t)(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) +X_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm alt}), $$

define the net-funding quantities

$$ S_t=\min(V_t,F_t),\qquad U_t^f=(V_t-F_t)^+,\qquad X_t^f=(F_t-V_t)^+. $$

For arbitrary \(r_{\rm alt}\), show that the exact reduced representation is

$$ \boxed{ S_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) +U_t^f(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) +X_t^f(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm alt}) +(S_t-C_t)^+(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm alt}) } $$

(inside the usual expectation, discount factor and time integral).

Explain the last term economically. When \(F_t>C_t\), some repo capacity classified as Channel 3 may still be used to finance this trade's own \(U_t\) rather than being genuinely external excess cash. If \(r_{\rm alt}\neq r_{\rm CoF}\), treating that amount at \(r_{\rm alt}\) rather than \(r_{\rm CoF}\) creates the correction

$$ (S_t-C_t)^+(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm alt}). $$

Under the standing base-case assumption

$$ r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}, $$

this correction vanishes and Proposition 2 collapses to the existing clean form

$$ \boxed{ S_t(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm base}) +U_t^f(r_{\rm CoF}-r_{\rm base}) +X_t^f(r_{\rm repo}-r_{\rm CoF}) }. $$

This is the case in which \(q_{\rm reg}\) disappears from the total, conditional on the collateral process \(B_t\). For arbitrary \(r_{\rm alt}\), \(C_t=\min(V_t,q_{\rm reg}B_t)\) remains in the correction term, so total ColVA can depend explicitly on \(q_{\rm reg}\).

Please update the surrounding explanation and sensitivity table accordingly. Make clear that \(r_{\rm alt}=r_{\rm CoF}\) is the standing/base-case economic assumption of the paper, not a mathematical necessity, and that the simple Proposition 2 is the specialization of the more general expression to that assumption.
