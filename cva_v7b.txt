1. USE THREE EQUATIONS AS THE CONCEPTUAL BACKBONE
======================================================================

Test the entire main document against the following minimal architecture.

First, pathwise CVA:

    CVA_0
      =
    LGD_SPV sum_i DF(0,t_i) Delta S_i
        E[(V_ti^uw - B_ti^uw)^+].

Second, collateral quantity immediately before the event:

    N_{t-}^post
      =
    min(
        V_{t-}/(q_CSA P_{t-}),
        N^pool
    ),

subject to the exact contractual CSA mechanics.

Third, unwind-state collateral value:

    B_t^uw
      =
    R_fn(
        N_{t-}^post,
        P_{t-},
        bond terms,
        default/restructuring state
    ),

with the proposed reduced-form first implementation:

    B_t^uw
      ≈
    R_eff N_{t-}^post.

Everything else in the methodology should have a clear purpose in explaining,
calibrating, implementing, validating or qualifying one of these equations.

If a section, symbol, stage or distinction does not materially help with one
of these objects, question whether it belongs in the main body.

3. REDUCE THE MODEL TO TWO ECONOMIC TIMES: tau- AND tau
======================================================================

Reconsider whether the current “Stage 1 / Stage 2a / Stage 2b / Stage 3”
architecture is genuinely helpful.

Conceptually, the baseline model mainly requires two states/times:

    tau-:
        immediately before the loss-trigger event;

    tau:
        the loss-trigger / contractual unwind state.

At tau-:

    N_{tau-}^post
      =
    min(
        N_{tau-}^req,
        N^pool
    ).

The pre-default sovereign credit path determines P_t and therefore how much
bond principal has become posted by tau-.

At tau:

    V_tau^uw
        = derivative unwind value;

    B_tau^uw
        = value of the posted collateral available against the unwind claim.

Then:

    E_tau
      =
    (V_tau^uw - B_tau^uw)^+.

This is the central event transition.

Do not create multiple formal “stages” merely to describe the two terms inside
this exposure equation.

If “stages” remain useful pedagogically, keep them very light. Otherwise
organize the sections directly around:

    CVA/exposure
    -> collateral quantity
    -> collateral unwind-state value
    -> derivative unwind value.

4. KEEP B^uw AS THE CENTRAL COLLATERAL OBJECT
======================================================================

Use:

    B_tau^uw

as the quantity entering CVA:

    E_tau = (V_tau^uw - B_tau^uw)^+.

Avoid maintaining a baseline chain such as:

    B^def -> B^disp -> B^uw

unless there are genuinely different economically material quantities that
must be modeled separately.

For the baseline, B_tau^uw should answer one practical question:

    What value of the posted sovereign bond is available to offset the
    derivative unwind claim at the loss-relevant event?

If event-state market value and disposal proceeds are assumed identical for
the baseline, there is no need for two separate symbols.

If an incremental disposal effect is later established, introduce it as a
refinement/sensitivity rather than part of the core model.

Likewise, “distress” belongs to the pre-event evolution of P_t and should not
replace the event-state concept.

5. KEEP THE TWO RECOVERIES, BUT EXPLAIN THEM ONCE
======================================================================

Preserve the distinction between:

    R_eff
        determines the value of the sovereign collateral at the event;

and:

    LGD_SPV
        applies to any residual unsecured derivative claim against the
        Repack vehicle after collateral has been credited.

This distinction is economically real and should NOT be removed merely for
simplicity.

Under the reduced-form collateral approximation:

    B_tau^uw
      ≈
    R_eff N_{tau-}^post,

so:

    exposure before SPV recovery
      =
    (V_tau^uw - R_eff N_{tau-}^post)^+.

Then LGD_SPV applies to that residual claim.

However, explain this distinction ONCE, cleanly.

Do not repeatedly revisit why R_eff and LGD are different.

If documentary analysis supports a limited-recourse assumption under which
little or no additional value remains after collateral is exhausted, then:

    LGD_SPV ≈ 1

is a reasonable modeling assumption.

State it concisely and condition it on the waterfall/ranking/recourse
documentation.

Do not make “LGD = 1” look like a mathematical trick used to move recovery
inside the exposure equation.

The economic interpretation is:

    sovereign recovery determines collateral value;

    SPV LGD determines recovery on the residual unsecured claim.

======================================================================
6. KEEP THE COLLATERAL CAP — THIS IS CORE, NOT OVERANALYSIS
======================================================================

Do NOT simplify away:

    N_t^post
      =
    min(
        V_t/(q_CSA P_t),
        N^pool
    ).

This is one of the genuinely important results.

It captures the fact that deterioration has two effects before default:

    P_t decreases
        ->
    each unit of bond is worth less;

but also:

    P_t decreases
        ->
    more bond principal is called under the CSA.

Before the cap binds, the second effect partially offsets the first.

Once:

    N_t^post = N^pool,

the collateral pool is exhausted and further deterioration directly reduces
coverage.

This is economically material and should remain in the core methodology.

But it should require only a concise explanation and perhaps the two-regime
formula; it does not require a large standalone apparatus.

======================================================================
7. SIMPLIFY THE “TWO PATHS” DISCUSSION
======================================================================

Retain the insight that:

    N_{tau-}^post

depends on the pre-default path.

A gradual deterioration path may result in:

    N_{tau-}^post = N^pool,

whereas a sufficiently discontinuous jump may result in:

    N_{tau-}^post < N^pool.

This is important.

However, do NOT create a separate “two-path model.”

In a full stochastic implementation, both are simply outcomes of:

    sovereign credit evolution
        ->
    P_t
        ->
    N_t^post
        ->
    tau.

Their probabilities should emerge endogenously from the modeled dynamics.

For the current worked example, if the model does not yet simulate these
dynamics fully, show:

    cap-bound case

and:

    jump-before-cap case

as scenarios/sensitivities demonstrating the materiality of N_{tau-}^post.

Do not assign arbitrary probabilities.

Do not overdevelop this section beyond what is required to establish the
path-dependence of posted collateral quantity.

    RMV

The main body needs only to establish:

    B_tau^uw
      =
    event-state value of the posted sovereign collateral,

and that this requires a chosen recovery/restructuring convention.

For the proposed reduced-form implementation:

    B_tau^uw
      ≈
    R_eff N_{tau-}^post,

where R_eff is interpreted as effective market value of the
recovery/restructuring consideration per unit of old principal.

Detailed RMV/RT/RFV mechanics, including Laurent's coupon-versus-principal
discounting result, should live primarily in the literature appendix.

Make explicit there that Laurent's discounting result concerns pre-default
pricing of a live defaultable bond and is distinct from the CVA event-state
valuation question.
