2. USE N^post RATHER THAN N^posted
============================================================

Replace:

    N_t^posted

with:

    N_t^post

throughout.

Likewise use:

    B_t^post

rather than:

    B_t^posted.

The core CSA relationship is:

    N_t^post = min(N_t^req, N_t^pool).

Most importantly, when determining collateral available at the loss-trigger
event, use:

    N_{tau-}^post

rather than N_tau^post.

This distinction is economically important.

The relevant quantity is the bond notional already posted immediately before
the event. A jump occurring at tau does not automatically cause additional
bond collateral to become posted at tau.

Therefore default-state collateral valuation should depend on:

    N_{tau-}^post.

============================================================
3. USE “UNWIND VALUE” AS THE PRIMARY DERIVATIVE TERMINOLOGY
============================================================

Replace:

    V_tau^co

with:

    V_tau^uw

throughout the methodology.

Define:

    V_tau^uw
        derivative unwind value at the loss-trigger event.

Use “unwind” as the primary terminology because the assumed transaction
mechanics are:

    collateral issuer default/restructuring
        ->
    derivative termination/unwind
        ->
    unwind value determined
        ->
    collateral disposal
        ->
    residual exposure.

“Close-out” may still be used where legally or technically appropriate,
particularly when discussing documentation, but the primary mathematical
notation should be V^uw.

This helps distinguish the transaction-specific Repack unwind mechanism from a
generic counterparty-default close-out framework.

============================================================
4. DO NOT USE “REAL” FOR COLLATERAL PROCEEDS
============================================================

Remove:

    B_tau^real

and avoid “real” or “realization” as mathematical superscripts/subscripts.

In a fixed-income document, particularly one whose illustrative collateral is
inflation-linked, “real” can naturally be interpreted as real versus nominal.

Instead use:

    B_tau^disp

for:

    collateral disposal value available to the dealer following the
    loss-trigger event.

Thus the exposure equation becomes:

    E_tau = (V_tau^uw - B_tau^disp)^+.

Use “disposal value” or “disposal proceeds” consistently in prose.

Avoid wording such as “ultimate recovery” where it could imply a workout value
received years after default. The relevant quantity is the economic value
available through the contractual unwind/disposal process.

============================================================
5. REPLACE q_liq / q_rel / q_res WITH q_disp
============================================================

Use:

    q_disp

throughout.

Define:

    q_disp
        incremental disposal adjustment applied after determining the
        default/restructuring-state collateral value.

Then:

    B_tau^disp = q_disp B_tau^def.

Do NOT define q_disp simply as a “liquidity haircut.”

Its purpose is broader.

It represents any incremental difference between the modeled
default/restructuring-state value B_tau^def and the disposal value available
to the dealer, for example:

- execution costs;
- disposal delay;
- market impact;
- transaction-specific liquidation effects;

but only to the extent these effects are NOT already reflected in B_tau^def.

The baseline should be:

    q_disp = 1.00.

Explain explicitly what this means.

q_disp = 1 does NOT mean:

    “the defaulted sovereign bond is perfectly liquid.”

It means:

    no separate disposal adjustment is applied beyond the modeled
    default/restructuring-state market value.

If B_tau^def or R_eff is calibrated from observable restructuring-package
market values, CDS auction/defaulted-security prices, distressed/default-state
market prices, or another measure that already reflects disposal conditions,
applying an additional q_disp < 1 could double count the same economic effect.

Use q_disp < 1 only where there is an identifiable incremental disposal effect
not already embedded in B_tau^def / R_eff.

Any q_disp < 1 case should therefore be presented as a modeling assumption or
sensitivity, not as an inherent consequence of sovereign default.

Do not reintroduce:

    B^real
    q_liq
    q_rel
    q_res

elsewhere in the paper.

8. PRESERVE AND SHARPEN THE §3 MESSAGE
============================================================

The current §3 logic is good and should remain.

While sufficient collateral is available:

    B_{tau-}^post
        ≈ V_{tau-}/q_CSA
        > V_{tau-}

for q_CSA < 1.

Therefore:

    (V_{tau-} - B_{tau-}^post)^+ ≈ 0.

Retain the important conclusion:

    this is a property of the margining mechanism,
    not evidence that default-state exposure is zero.

Then transition directly to:

    E_tau = (V_tau^uw - B_tau^disp)^+.

Make the distinction between tau- and tau visually prominent.

The reader should understand the sequence:

    immediately before the event:
        performing CSA margining may make exposure approximately zero

    at the event:
        the derivative is unwound and the sovereign bond enters its
        default/restructuring state

    following the event:
        the relevant collateral quantity is its disposal value,
        B_tau^disp

    therefore:
        B_{tau-}^post cannot simply be substituted for B_tau^disp.

9. KEEP THE COLLATERAL-QUANTITY DYNAMICS CLEAR
============================================================

Preserve the distinction among:

(a) the total collateral pool held/available;
(b) collateral actually posted under the CSA;
(c) assets legally available to satisfy the dealer's unwind claim.

The current draft correctly states that these do not necessarily coincide.

Do not describe the €51m pool simply as static “overcollateralization.”

The mechanism is dynamic:

    bond credit deterioration
        ->
    P_t falls
        ->
    N_t^req increases
        ->
    progressively more bond notional may be posted
        ->
    N_t^post may eventually reach N_pool.

Thus:

    N_t^post = min(N_t^req, N_t^pool).

For the worked example, distinguish clearly between:

    N_0^post

and:

    N_pool = €51m.

If a default-state example assumes:

    N_{tau-}^post = N_pool = €51m,

state the assumption explicitly:

    [MODEL CHOICE]
    The collateral cap is assumed to have become binding during the
    pre-default deterioration, so the entire available pool is posted
    immediately before the loss-trigger event.

Do not imply that a jump occurring at tau itself causes the remaining pool to
become posted.

10. FINAL NOTATION AND CORE ECONOMIC CHAIN
============================================================

After these revisions, the main notation should be internally consistent:

    N^pool       available bond pool notional
    N^req        CSA-required bond notional
    N^post       actually posted bond notional

    B^pool       market value of available bond pool
    B^post       market value of posted bond

    V^uw         derivative unwind value

    B^def        default/restructuring-state value of posted bond
    B^disp       collateral disposal value available to dealer

    q_CSA        contractual CSA valuation percentage
    R_eff        effective default/restructuring value per unit face
    q_disp       incremental disposal adjustment.

The core economic sequence should read:

    credit-state-dependent P_t
        ->
    N_{tau-}^post
        ->
    B_tau^def
        ->
    B_tau^disp = q_disp B_tau^def
        ->
    E_tau = (V_tau^uw - B_tau^disp)^+.

The CVA expression then uses:

    CVA_0
      = LGD Σ_i DF(0,t_i)
          E[(V_ti^uw - B_ti^disp)^+]
          ΔS_i.
